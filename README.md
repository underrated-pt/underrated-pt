Welcome to Underrated Pony Town!
You may have noticed lots of Pony Town Awards Accounts starting to pop up recently. And Strawberry Cream here has noticed something. There isn't a page for lesser known players to appreciate! We all know the path players, but lets take the time to appreciate someone YOU think deserves some spotlight! 

Now, I'm not talking about the ones everyone knows. I mean the players who don't get talked about! Someone you've NEVER seen get an award, someone you admire, someone no one talks about! The same players are always talked about, so lets shine some spotlight on someone who never gets it!

Strawberry Cream wants people who work hard to make their ponies or githubs look nice! People who may not have many friends on here, or who don't have many followers! Someone who doesn't sit on the path on the daily, someone we don't know yet! 
<p align="center">

</p>
Here is how we'll do this. Send Strawberry Cream the pony player you want to suggest through the atabook linked! https://underrated.atabook.org/

Please tell us as much details to find this player! Tell us where they are likely to be spotted, that way Strawberry can have a look for their self! We only ask that players do not nominate themselves. Strawberry's reasoning for this is so that the community will go out to find others they can appreciate! On top of that, anyone that is considered problematic will not be added. If someone is added that is considered as such, please inform Strawberry Cream! Have anything else to say? Strawberry cream is open to suggestions! @11no11mo on discord! 

As of now, Strawberry Cream is waiting for suggested players, they ask for your patience!
